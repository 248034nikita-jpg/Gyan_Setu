Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an email?

Yes, email is required to create your account.

How many children can I add?

Free plan: 1 child. Premium plans allow more children.

How do kids earn coins?

By playing games and completing learning activities.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes! No ads, no strangers, no external links.

What can kids do with coins?

Children can spend coins on rewards and items in the shop.

Can I track my child's progress?

Yes! Parent dashboard shows detailed progress and activity.
